XT DOS boot files

Extracted from boot/freedos_hd.img in VirtualXT 3.1.0, commit 2aecaf13:
https://git.libretro.com/libretro/virtualxt

Contains the FreeDOS MBR/boot sector, KERNEL.SYS, FreeCOM COMMAND.COM,
CuteMouse CTMOUSE.EXE, Lo-tech LTEMM.EXE and Use!UMBs USE!UMBS.SYS.
The archive does not include the demonstration games from the original disk.

FreeDOS and source packages: https://www.freedos.org/
